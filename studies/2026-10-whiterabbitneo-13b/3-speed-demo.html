How fast does a model answer?

The same question given to each model, measured on a Mac mini (Apple M6, 32 GB). This is a replay at the real timings: each piece of text appears exactly when it arrived during the measurement. Nothing is sped up.

0.0 s
Tokens per second (tok/s) is how many pieces of text (roughly three quarters of a word each) the model writes per second. At 15 tok/s you can read along without waiting; at 30 it is faster than most people read.
